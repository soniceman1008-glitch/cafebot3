About Cafe Aroma

Cafe Aroma has been the neighborhood's favorite stop for coffee and conversation. We roast, brew, and bake with care, using quality ingredients and recipes we're proud of.

Whether you're grabbing a quick espresso on the way to work or settling in with a pastry and a good book, we're here to make your visit warm and welcoming.
